# Korean Professor Research Discovery — Source-Audited Playbook

Purpose: investigate Korean AI/CS faculty for undergraduate research collaboration and GKS-related outreach, with assumption-focused falsifiable hypotheses. This is documentation, not an automation.

**Each research run MUST read, in order:** PROTOCOL.md, HISTORICAL_CASES.md, LEDGER.md, REPORT_TEMPLATE.md. Obtain the full primary paper, official faculty page, supplements/code and comparable prior art. Missing source = mark unverified, not guess.

**Mindset:** Identify a real paper's specific representational assumption; construct controlled pairs that expose potential failure while holding confounders, budget and inputs constant. Do not equate impressive narrative with novelty.

**Outreach:** Check current university and GKS track eligibility independently. Never email, edit files, create tasks, or assert supervision availability without express authorization. Historical cases were discussed or mailed; NOT experimentally validated or guaranteed original.
