# Meno-J Falsification Engine

**Meno-J: A Falsification-Oriented Architecture for AI** treats speculation as input, not as the core contribution.

The engine accepts candidate claims from any source—human experts, language models, literature, prior experiments, or automated search—and subjects them to a disciplined sequence:

```text
Candidate intake
→ contract normalization
→ strict mechanism filtering
→ cross-candidate pattern extraction
→ rival explanation generation
→ falsification design
→ deterministic execution and validation
→ working-theory update
```

The historical `Dreamer` remains available as one replaceable candidate-intake adapter. The architecture does not depend on it and does not treat fluent hypothesis generation as scientific success.

See [the architecture document](outputs/meno_j_falsification_engine_architecture.md) for the evidence, component boundaries, terminology mapping, and operating rules.

Existing experiment outputs are immutable evidence. Architecture reframing does not rename, rewrite, or invalidate them.

The theory-only continuation is [Theory Study 1: What Makes an Explanation Genuinely Good?](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_1_explanation_quality.md). It rejects a tempting universal “counterfactual compression” account and replaces it with a validated, six-gate **Meno-J Explanation Contract**. The study also gives the J-jump a concrete standard: a genuine jump must change the representation, retain prior warranted successes or expose their boundary, gain new discriminating reach, control what it omits, beat serious rivals, and create new ways to fail. See the [frozen protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_1_protocol.md), [independent validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_1_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_1_reproducibility_summary.md).

The first historical calibration is [Theory Study 2: Ptolemy → Kepler → Newton](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_2_ptolemy_kepler_newton.md). It found a real defect in the initial J-jump rule: Kepler can account for Ptolemy's success without clearly retaining enough Ptolemaic theoretical machinery. The validated [historical amendment](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_2_contract_amendment.md) therefore replaces literal retention with **retention accounting**, requires component-scoped verdicts, separates conceptual from computational compression, records approximation boundaries, and preserves observationally equivalent rivals. The result classifies Kepler's orbital representation as a surviving J-jump while rejecting his magnetic mechanism, and classifies Newtonian dynamics as a deeper surviving J-jump with explicit historical loose ends. See the [protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_2_j_jump_casebook_protocol.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_2_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_2_reproducibility_summary.md).

The cross-domain calibration is [Theory Study 3: Miasma, Sanitation, Transmission, and Germ Theories](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_3_miasma_germ_casebook.md). It shows that the medical transition was not one arrow or one hero's discovery: Snow supplied a cholera route without a germ identification, Semmelweis supplied a contact intervention with a contested full etiology, Pasteur supplied microbial ontology and contamination controls, and Koch supplied disease-specific causal triangulation. All five Study 2 amendments generalized, but the validated [contract extension](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_3_contract_extension.md) now models J-jumps as distributed lineage graphs, decouples intervention from mechanism, requires an evidence-triangulation ladder, declares relational causal roles, and separates discovery, uptake, implementation, and outcome. See the [protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_3_miasma_germ_protocol.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_3_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_3_reproducibility_summary.md).

The layered-physics calibration is [Theory Study 4: Caloric, Thermodynamics, and Kinetic-Statistical Theory](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_4_caloric_kinetic_casebook.md). It rejects the binary “caloric became kinetic theory” framing. The actual successor combines a quantitative energy-conversion law, autonomous macroscopic thermodynamic constraints, and a later molecular-statistical bridge. Caloric's substance ontology failed while some calorimetric mathematics and Carnot's engine constraint survived reinterpretation. The validated [contract extension](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_4_contract_extension.md) therefore separates formal structure from ontology, distinguishes constraints from mechanisms, requires explicit cross-level bridge laws, labels transition roles before assigning J-jump credit, and permits mutually consistent layered successors. See the [protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_4_caloric_kinetic_protocol.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_4_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_4_reproducibility_summary.md).

The historical-geoscience calibration is [Theory Study 5: Continental Drift to Plate Tectonics](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_5_continental_drift_plate_tectonics_casebook.md). It separates an idea being worthy of pursuit from being ready for acceptance, blocks hindsight from crediting Wegener with later evidence, and shows that plate tectonics redefined the moving object from continents to lithospheric plates rather than merely adding a mechanism. The validated [contract extension](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_5_contract_extension.md) adds a hindsight firewall, an epistemic-stance ladder, an object-redefinition test, an independent-prediction ledger, and an observability frontier. See the [protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_5_continental_drift_plate_tectonics_protocol.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_5_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_5_reproducibility_summary.md).

The twenty historically calibrated rules are now executable in [Theory Study 6: Minimal Executable Epistemic Scoring](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_6_executable_scoring_results.md). The engine uses no aggregate score: it independently decides rejection, pursuit, bounded provisional use, and acceptance before assigning one primary stance. Five predeclared controls reached all five stances exactly once, while eight adversarial mutations blocked hindsight leakage, duplicate-evidence inflation, unresolved object identity, and the conversion of predictive usefulness into explanatory truth. See the [frozen protocol](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_6_executable_scoring_protocol.md), [independent validation](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_6_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_theory_study_6_reproducibility_summary.md).

The latest deterministic follow-up is [Experiment 12: Temporal Readiness-Surface Replication](outputs/meno_j_experiment_12_temporal_readiness_surface.md). Under disjoint early/middle/late temporal holdouts, subject normalization again improved accuracy—this time by 0.1751—and reduced full-label sets by 0.1237. S4's large repair replicated in every temporal block, while S2 remained unresolved. The order-statistic transition was unsafe under all four representations, the proposed surface interaction was not supported, and the earlier temperature-ablation result did not replicate. See the [working-theory update](outputs/meno_j_experiment_12_working_theory_update.md), [independent validation](outputs/meno_j_experiment_12_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_experiment_12_reproducibility_summary.md).

The latest external falsification is [Experiment 13: Independent Wearable-Dataset Replication](outputs/meno_j_experiment_13_external_replication.md). The user-supplied official PhysioNet archive passed SHA-256 and full CRC validation, and the frozen 33-subject, 198-fold protocol passed independent exact replay. Normalization modestly improved accuracy by 0.0436 and reduced full-label sets by 0.0909, but missed the preregistered +0.08 accuracy threshold and lowered mean coverage to 0.8681, below the 0.88 safety floor. The external replication was therefore not supported. See the [working-theory update](outputs/meno_j_experiment_13_working_theory_update.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_experiment_13_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_experiment_13_reproducibility_summary.md).

The latest mechanistic follow-up is [Experiment 14: Class-Conditional Calibration Repair](outputs/meno_j_experiment_14_class_conditional_repair.md). Across 396 validated folds, class-Mondrian calibration reduced the normalized class-coverage gap from 0.0972 to 0.0114, showing that threshold pooling contributed to the asymmetry. It did not complete the safety repair: mean coverage reached only 0.8769, stress coverage remained 0.8712, and worst-subject coverage fell to 0.6875. The leading unresolved mechanism is now subject-level reliability or distribution shift rather than class pooling alone. See the [working-theory update](outputs/meno_j_experiment_14_working_theory_update.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_experiment_14_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_experiment_14_reproducibility_summary.md).

The literature-grounded continuation is [Experiment 15: Confidence–Trust Subject-Shift Diagnostic](outputs/meno_j_experiment_15_confidence_trust_diagnostic.md), selected after a [19-source primary-paper review](outputs/meno_j_literature_review_after_experiment_14.md). Across 3,168 heldout observations, the normalized class-Mondrian arm covered only 0.7852 of high-confidence/low-trust windows, but high-confidence/high-trust coverage was nearly identical at 0.7953. The frozen trust-specific check therefore failed: confidence marked the small-set risk region, while the geometric trust score added only a 0.0100 miscoverage separation. The result blocks a confidence–trust subject gate and redirects the engine toward protocol/stressor posterior shift or temporal dependence. See the [working-theory update](outputs/meno_j_experiment_15_working_theory_update.md), [validation](outputs/meno_j_experiment_15_validation.md), and [reproducibility summary](outputs/meno_j_experiment_15_reproducibility_summary.md).
